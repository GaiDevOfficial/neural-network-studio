is allowed when the AGPL obligations are met.

A separate commercial license is available for organizations that need terms the AGPL does not provide,
for example:

- Incorporating Node Studio into a proprietary product without the AGPL's source-disclosure obligations.
- Offering a modified Node Studio as a hosted service without the AGPL's network-source obligation.
- Obtaining a written commercial warranty, support or indemnity.
- Using the Node Studio name or branding in a commercial offering.

Commercial licensing does not transfer ownership of the original project or its trademarks. It grants
specific rights defined in a written agreement.

To discuss a commercial license, contact the maintainer through the GitHub profile
[@GaiDevOfficial](https://github.com/GaiDevOfficial) with:

- Your organization and the intended product.
- Whether you need redistribution, hosted-service or internal-use rights.
- The expected scale and support requirements.

This document is a summary, not a license. Only a signed commercial agreement grants commercial terms
beyond the AGPL.
